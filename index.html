The Thesis

Content
is the
foundation

Find one video that works. Run with it. Build audience before you touch playlisting.

DKD started with 45 Spotify monthly listeners and no social footprint. Instead of throwing money at Spotify playlists immediately, we went social-first — finding a niche audience, spending efficiently on repeatable impressions, and building a platform strong enough to unlock editorial placements.

The entire campaign ran over 12 months. The playbook is repeatable. This is the proof of concept.

Core Insight

A niche audience that converts beats a broad audience that scrolls past. Chinese-American listeners were the unlock.

Phase One — Social First

Niche. Own It.

Most artists try to reach everyone. We went the opposite way — targeting Chinese and Chinese-American audiences specifically, because that community converts.

Paid social on Instagram and TikTok with tight demographic targeting. The unit economics were clean: $1 per Instagram follower. We reinvested until we hit 11K.

Why It Worked

Niche + repetition + dollars with clear CPF = compounding audience. The Chinese diaspora music community was undersaturated and highly engaged.

What Stalled It

Missed
Opportunities

Growth stalled after SAMO disengaged. Some of this was expected. Some was preventable. These are the variables that, if activated, would have compounded the momentum significantly.

Missed
Not Enough Content
The content machine needed to run hotter. One viral video is a start, not a system. Consistent output would have sustained algorithm reach without relying purely on paid spend.
Missed
No Artist Collabs
Spotify Radio revealed a cluster of adjacent artists with overlapping audiences. Collaboration tracks — even loosies — would have cross-pollinated listeners and created organic playlist adjacency.
Missed
No Local Presence
No local shows. No community building in the city. Live presence converts passive Spotify listeners into real fans. It's the multiplier the algorithm can't replicate.
Missed
No Asia Shows
After landing a genuine Taiwanese chart position and a Chinese-diaspora audience, the logical next step was Asia shows. A short Asia run could have converted that digital footprint into real ticket revenue.
Missed
No Active Steering
Streams have held since the agency disengaged — Discovery Mode is doing its job. But no new growth. Momentum without a hand on the wheel eventually levels off.
What Held
Discovery Mode Maintained
Distrokid's Discovery Mode continues to generate algorithmic plays. The playlist footprint (50+ playlists) has provided a baseline stream floor even without new campaigns.
The Playbook

Repeatable
Blueprint

This campaign is a proof of concept. The framework scales. Here's what we know works.

Step 01
Find Your Niche
Identify an underserved, high-affinity community for the artist. For DKD it was Chinese-diaspora listeners — culturally resonant, digitally active, and willing to follow. Niche audiences convert better than broad ones.
Step 02
Spend on Repetition
Paid social with tight targeting. Goal: $1 CPF or better. Spend until you have 10K+ social followers. That number is the key to editorial. Don't move to Spotify spend until you have social proof.
Step 03
Hit Curator Campaigns
$500 per curator campaign. Run multiple rounds across different tracks. Target until songs enter Discovery Mode. Three Spotify Ads campaigns running in parallel maximize the surface area.
Step 04
Pitch Editorial
10K+ Instagram + 20K monthly listeners + chart data = a pitch that lands. Apple Music editorial, Spotify editorial pitches, and blog coverage all become accessible at this threshold. Not before.
Step 05 — Missed Opportunity
Build IRL Presence
Shows in the niche market. For DKD, Asia was the obvious next step. Local shows wherever the artist lives. Live builds what digital can't: real fans, ticket buyers, word-of-mouth.
Proof of Concept
It Works in 12 Months
45 listeners to 20K. $0 to $14K spend. Zero social presence to 11K IG followers and an Apple Music placement. The blueprint is validated. The only question is whether the artist stays in the car.
